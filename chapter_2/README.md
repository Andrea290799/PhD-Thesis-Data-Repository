- **Supplementary Table 2.5: Summary of differentially expressed miRNAs (DEMs, |log₂(FC)| ≥ 2 and _p_-value < 0.05) in PL-EVs across the three experimental comparisons**. The table contains information about the miRNA name, its log₂(FC) value in the given comparison (column comp: s_m, S vs. M; s_c, S vs. C; m_c, M vs. C), its log₂ transformed mean expression value across all the samples, statistical metrics (moderated t-statistic, raw _p_-value, and _p_-adjusted value, B-statistic), total number of comparisons in which the miRNA reached significance as a DEM and specifies the corresponding experimental groups. DEMs are ordered in descending order of |log₂(FC)|. Associated with Figure 2.3 and Figure A.2.
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.6: Summary of differentially expressed proteins (DEPs, |log₂(FC)| ≥ 2 and _p_-adjusted value < 0.05) in PL-EVs across the three experimental comparisons**. The table contains information about the protein name, its entrez ID, its Uniport ID, its log₂(FC) value in the given comparison (column comp: s_m, S vs. M; s_c, S vs. C; m_c, M vs. C), statistical metrics (raw _p_-value, and _p_-adjusted value), total number of comparisons in which the protein reached significance as a DEP and specifies the corresponding experimental groups. Targeting experimentally validated DEMs (either in the same or in a different comparison) are also specified. DEPs are ordered in descending order of |log₂(FC)|. Associated with Figure 2.3 and Figure A.2.
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.7: Summary of Spearman correlated (_ρ_ ≥ 0.8 and _p_-adjusted value < 0.05) miRNAs and proteins in PL-EVs across different allergic severity grades**. The table, for each interaction pair, provides: the molecule names, the correlation score _ρ_, _p_-value, _p_-adjusted value, and information about if the miRNA, protein or both are DE. "Is target?" column indicates if the interaction has been experimentally validated. Correlated pairs are soretd in descending order of |Correlation score|. Associated with Figure 2.4. 
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.8: Functional ORA enrichment results for PL-EV differentially expressed molecules**. This table provides the results of the GO ORA enrichment analyses for differentially expressed miRNAs and proteins (independently; indicated in cluster Column) across the three studied comparisons (comp: s_m, S vs. M; s_c, S vs. C; m_c, M vs. C). For each identified term, the table specifies the functional ontology (ONTOLOGY) along with the corresponding GO identifier and description (ID/Description). The magnitude of enrichment is detailed through the GeneRatio, background ratio (BgRatio), RichFactor, FoldEnrichment, and zScore metrics. Statistical significance is rigorously defined by the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The table also lists the total number of involved molecules (Count), their specific symbols (geneID), and the high-level hierarchical category (parentTerm) used to group specific GO terms into the top-level biological processes described in the study. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by RichFactor. Associated with Figure 2.5. 
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.9: Results of the joint miRNA-protein functional ORA enrichment analysis**. This table details the results of the integrated ORA enrichment analysis identifying biological processes where both DEMs and DEPs converge. Each entry includes the comparison (Cluster: s_m, S vs. M; s_c, S vs. C; m_c, M vs. C), the functional ontology (ONTOLOGY), the unique GO identifier (ID), and a detailed description of the biological process or molecular function. The magnitude and statistical significance of the enrichment are provided through the GeneRatio, background ratio (BgRatio), RichFactor, FoldEnrichment, and zScore metrics, alongside the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The specific symbols for the miRNA and protein members involved (geneID) are also provided. Finally, specific GO terms are mapped to their respective hierarchical categories (parentTerm) to facilitate the top-level biological process grouping discussed in the manuscript. Top-level GO terms are only annotated for those GO terms represented in the figure. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by RichFactor. Associated with Figure 2.6. 
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.10: GO term GSEA of miRNAs in PL-EVs**. This table presents the results of the GSEA performed on the global expression profiles of detected miRNAs. The analysis independently considers the 3’ and 5’ arms of the miRNAs across the studied comparisons to characterize their collective impact on biological functions. For each functional category, the table lists the comparison (Cluster, indicated also here the considered miRNA arm: s_m3 or s_m5, S vs. M; s_c3 or s_c5, S vs. C; m_c3 or m_c5, M vs. C), ontology type (ONTOLOGY), and specific GO identifier and description (ID, Description). The magnitude of functional association is quantified through the setSize, enrichmentScore, and NES, while statistical reliability is documented via the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. Additional columns specify the rank of maximum enrichment, the leading_edge components, and the core_enrichment molecules, with all specific terms mapped to high-level hierarchical categories (parentTerm) for functional grouping. Top-level GO terms are only annotated for those GO terms represented in the figure. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by |NES|. 
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.11: GSEA of miRNA regulatory targets in PL-EVs**. This table provides the results for the GSEA of miRNA targets across all comparisons. For each enriched miRNA, the table specifies the comparison (Cluster: s_m, S vs. M; s_c, S vs. C; m_c, M vs. C), and the miRNA name (ID, Description). Functional impact is quantified through the setSize, the raw enrichmentScore, and the NES. Statistical significance is reported via raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The table also details the rank where the enrichment score was maximized, the specific components of the leading_edge (tags, list, and signal), and the identifiers of the molecules comprising the core_enrichment, with core-count representing the number of molecules in core_enrichment. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by |NES|. 
  
- **Supplementary Table 2.12: ORA of regulated miRNA targets in PL-EVs**. This table presents the results of the ORA each performed on the different miRNA target groups of highly regulated miRNAs. Each entry lists the miRNA that regulated all the targets used for the ORA (Cluster), functional ontology (ONTOLOGY), and the specific GO identifier and description (ID, Description). The magnitude of functional enrichment is quantified through the GeneRatio, background ratio (BgRatio), RichFactor, FoldEnrichment and zScore metrics. Statistical reliability is established via the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. Furthermore, the table specifies the total number of target genes (Count) and their specific identifiers (geneID) associated with each term. Results are sorted first by miRNA, then by RichFactor.
