- **Supplementary Table 2.11: Results of separate enrichment ORAs of the PL-EV differentially expressed molecules**. This table provides the results of the GO ORA enrichment analyses for differentially expressed miRNAs and proteins (independently; indicated in cluster Column) across the three studied comparisons (comp: s_m, severe vs. mild; s_c, severe vs. control; m_c, mild vs. control). For each identified term, the table specifies the functional ontology (ONTOLOGY) along with the corresponding GO identifier and description (ID/Description). The magnitude of enrichment is detailed through the GeneRatio, background ratio (BgRatio), RichFactor, FoldEnrichment, and zScore metrics. Statistical significance is rigorously defined by the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The table also lists the total number of involved molecules (Count), their specific symbols (geneID), and the high-level hierarchical category (parentTerm) used to group specific GO terms into the top-level biological processes described in the study. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by RichFactor. Associated with Figure 2.4.

-  **Supplementary Table 2.12: Results of enrichment ORA of the combined PL-EV differentially expressed molecules**. This table details the results of the integrated ORA enrichment analysis identifying biological processes where both DEMs and DEPs converge. Each entry includes the comparison (Cluster: s_m, severe vs. mild; s_c, severe vs. control; m_c, mild vs. control), the functional ontology (ONTOLOGY), the unique GO identifier (ID), and a detailed description of the biological process or molecular function. The magnitude and statistical significance of the enrichment are provided through the GeneRatio, background ratio (BgRatio), RichFactor, FoldEnrichment, and zScore metrics, alongside the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The specific symbols for the miRNA and protein members involved (geneID) are also provided. Finally, specific GO terms are mapped to their respective hierarchical categories (parentTerm) to facilitate the top-level biological process grouping discussed in the manuscript. Top-level GO terms are only annotated for those GO terms represented in the figure. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by RichFactor. Associated with Figure 2.5. 

- **Supplementary Table 2.13: Summary of Spearman correlated (_ρ_ ≥ 0.8 and _p_-adjusted value < 0.05) miRNAs and proteins in PL-EVs across different allergic severity grades**. The table, for each interaction pair, provides: the molecule names, the correlation score _ρ_, _p_-value, _p_-adjusted value, and information about if the miRNA, protein or both are DE. "Is target?" column indicates if the interaction has been experimentally validated. Correlated pairs are soretd in descending order of |Correlation score|. Associated with Figure 2.6. 

- **Supplementary Table 2.14: Protein expression-based miRNA GSEA.** This table provides the results for the GSEA of miRNA targets across all comparisons. For each enriched miRNA, the table specifies the comparison (Cluster: s_m, severe vs. mild; s_c, severe vs. control; m_c, mild vs. control), and the miRNA name (ID, Description). Functional impact is quantified through the setSize, the raw enrichmentScore, and the NES. Statistical significance is reported via raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The table also details the rank where the enrichment score was maximized, the specific components of the leading_edge (tags, list, and signal), and the identifiers of the molecules comprising the core_enrichment, with core-count representing the number of molecules in core_enrichment. Results are sorted first by comparison, then by |NES|.  Associated with Figure 2.7.

- **Supplementary Table 2.15: Reactome enrichment analysis of proteins encoded by target genes of GSEA-enriched miRNAs.** This table provides the results across all comparisons. For each enriched Reactome pathway, the table specifies the enriched miRNA (Cluster), the pathway identifier and description (ID/Description). The magnitude of enrichment is detailed through the GeneRatio, background ratio (BgRatio), RichFactor, FoldEnrichment, and zScore metrics. Statistical significance is rigorously defined by the raw _p_-value, Benjamini-Hochberg _p_-adjusted value (p.adjust), and q-value. The table also lists the total number of involved molecules (Count), their specific symbols (geneID). Results are sorted first by Count. Associated with Figure 2.7.
